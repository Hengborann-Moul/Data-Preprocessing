## Key Takeaways
* **Engagement** is the most dominant state, present in roughly **94%** of the clips.
* **Boredom** is the second most common, appearing in about **25%** of the cases.
* **Confusion** and **Frustration** are relatively rare events, occurring in less than **10%** and **5%** of the dataset respectively.
